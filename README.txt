HARSHALI JAGTAP - PORTFOLIO WEBSITE

HOW TO USE:
1. Open index.html in Chrome/Edge to preview the website.
2. Replace the placeholder college/university information.
3. Replace your.email@example.com with your real email.
4. Replace the # links for GitHub and LinkedIn with your profile links.
5. Add your real resume as: resume/resume.pdf
6. To add your photo, replace the profile-placeholder section in index.html with:
   <img src="images/profile.jpg" alt="Harshali Jagtap">
7. Replace/add project links, certificates and achievements.

FILES:
- index.html  = website structure
- style.css   = design and responsive layout
- script.js   = animations, typing effect, menu and interactions
- images/     = place profile/project images here
- resume/     = place your PDF resume here

IMPORTANT:
The contact form is a visual demo. To receive messages, connect it to a form service or backend.
